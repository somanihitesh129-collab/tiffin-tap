CLEAR FROM ORDER TO COLLECTION

Here's how Tiffin Tap
will work.

A proposed partner-kitchen model designed around school collection points.

01
📱

Customer places an order

Parent or student selects a meal and enters the student's school and class details.

ORDER
02
🏫

Orders are grouped by school

Orders are organised by participating school to make fulfilment easier to coordinate.

SORT
03
📋

Quantities go to partner kitchens

Confirmed quantities and meal requirements are shared with the relevant kitchen partner.

COORDINATE
04
👩‍🍳

Meals are prepared and labelled

Partner kitchens prepare the meals, which are individually labelled for identification.

PREPARE
05
🛵

Tiffin Tap coordinates delivery

Meals are transported to an authorised collection point under the agreed school arrangement.

DELIVER
06
🍱

Student collects the tiffin

The student collects their labelled meal from the designated counter or authorised person.

COLLECT
07
♻️

Empty reusable box is returned

Returned boxes are tracked, collected for cleaning and sanitisation, and prepared for reuse.

REUSE
💡
Why partner kitchens first?

Instead of building its own kitchen at the start, Tiffin Tap proposes working with existing food-preparation partners. This can reduce the initial setup burden, but food safety, quality checks, capacity, pricing and delivery agreements must be verified before launch.

THE REUSABLE TIFFIN CYCLE

One box. A clear return loop.

💳1. Deposit paid

Refundable deposit collected for the first box.

🏷️2. Box identified

Each tiffin gets a unique ID or QR code.

🍽️3. Meal enjoyed

Student uses the labelled reusable tiffin.

↩️4. Box returned

Empty tiffin goes to the collection counter.

🧼5. Cleaned properly

Boxes are collected for cleaning and sanitisation.

💚6. Deposit refunded

Refund follows the final return policy.

Deposit amount, cleaning process, tracking method and return policy are not yet finalised.

Tiffin Tap is being developed by The First Huddle as a student entrepreneurship project.

OUR MISSION

Make lunch easier.
Make choices count.

Our mission is to make nutritious, convenient and affordable school meals more accessible to students while making lunch arrangements easier for parents.

We are starting with a focused idea: understand the lunch needs of school students, work with suitable kitchen partners, and build a dependable school-based ordering and collection process.

Tiffin Tap is currently a proposed early-stage business concept. Operations, partners, school permissions, prices and food-safety procedures must be confirmed before a live launch.
